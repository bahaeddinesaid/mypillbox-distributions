# MyPillbox — the family medication reminder

<p>
  <strong>Never miss a dose.</strong><br>
  A medication reminder built for seniors and the family that cares about them:
  the patient's phone rings at dose time, and one QR scan puts the whole family
  in the loop — everyone sees what was taken, and what wasn't.
</p>

- **Works in three languages:** العربية · Français · English (full interfaces, not translations bolted on)
- **Smart box scan:** photograph a medicine box once — the name, form and pill count are read automatically
- **Family circle:** relatives see the schedule, confirm doses, get a live nudge when a reminder fires
- **Stock tracking:** pills left in every box, with a warning before one runs dry
- **Offline-first:** everything works without a network; sync happens when it returns
- **Your server, your data:** the family runs its own small server. No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no cloud vendor reading your medicines

## The project

MyPillbox is a family project, written for a real father and his children —
designed with seniors in mind from the first screen: large text, high
contrast, one-touch confirmations, and reminders that ring loud and clear.
